Friends are better in person.

FWENDS is a social app where you earn collectible items and shared memory blocks by actually meeting up. No likes, no follower counts. Just proof you showed up for each other.

what is fwends

A social app that only counts the time you spend together.

Most apps reward you for scrolling. FWENDS rewards you for leaving the house. Everything you earn in it comes from real, in-person time with the people you care about.

irl only

Meet in person

A meetup in FWENDS is logged face to face, phone to phone. There's nothing to earn from the couch, and that's the point.

shared

Memory blocks

Every meetup becomes a memory block that both of you keep: when it happened, who was there, and a note about what you got up to.

earned

Collectibles

Showing up earns you collectible items. They can't be bought, only earned by spending time together, so every one has a story behind it.

how it works

Four steps, and most of them happen offline.

  1. Make your fwend

    Sign up with your phone number, claim a username, and set up the little character that represents you in the app.

  2. Add your people

    Send requests to the friends you actually want to see. FWENDS is built for your real circle, not an audience.

  3. Link up in person

    When you're together, one of you shows a code and the other scans it. That's how FWENDS knows you really met.

  4. Keep what you earned

    You both get a shared memory block, plus collectibles that grow with every meetup. Then put the phone away.

why it matters

Your phone should get you out the door, not keep you on the couch.

Group chats are easy to keep up with and it's still easy to drift apart. FWENDS turns seeing each other into something you can see, collect, and look back on.

  • friends who keep saying "we should hang out"
  • people new to a city
  • roommates & teammates
  • long-distance friends on a visit
  • anyone tired of the feed

  • Rewards real-world time

    Nothing in FWENDS can be earned by scrolling, posting, or chasing likes. Only meeting up counts.

  • Small circles, by design

    No followers and no public counts. Just the friends you've actually met, and the history you share.

  • Memories you own together

    A memory block belongs to everyone who was there, so you look back on it together, not just from one side.

  • Quiet controls

    You choose who you connect with, and you can remove or block someone quietly at any time.
