Frequently asked questions

Where can I download FWENDS?

FWENDS for iPhone is still in development and isn't on the App Store yet. Join the waitlist and we'll email you when it's available.

How does FWENDS know we met in person?

When you're together, one of you opens a code in the app and the other scans it with their phone. Linking up is designed to happen face to face, so the meetups in FWENDS reflect real time spent together.

What is a memory block?

A memory block is the shared record of a meetup: when it happened, who was there, and an optional note. Everyone who was there keeps the same memory block.

What are collectibles?

Collectibles are in-app items you earn by meeting up. They can't be bought or sold, and they have no cash value. They're there to mark the time you spent together.

How do I remove or block someone?

Open their profile in the app to remove them as a fwend or block them. Blocking is quiet: they aren't notified, and they can no longer find you or send you requests.

How do I delete my account?

You will be able to delete your account from Settings inside the app. You can also email hello@fwends.co with the subject “Delete my account” and your username. We'll verify the request and delete your account and its data as described in our Privacy Policy.

Staying safe when you meet up
  • Meet people you already know and trust.
  • Choose public places, and tell someone where you'll be.
  • You never have to scan a code or link up with anyone you don't want to.
  • If you are in danger, contact local emergency services first.
